SMS Consent & Messaging Terms

Last updated: July 2026

This messaging program is not currently active. Design Dept. Partners LLC is not sending SMS messages at this time, and there is no number to opt in to. This page documents the terms that will govern the program if and when we resume it.

The terms below describe the SMS messaging program operated by Design Dept. Partners LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). Questions about this page can be sent to hello@designdept.com.

Express Consent

If the program resumes, you would provide express consent to receive SMS messages from us by texting our published messaging number directly. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

How You Would Opt In

Opting in requires you to initiate contact — you would text our published messaging number first. No purchase or account creation is required. We do not add phone numbers to a messaging program from any other source, and we do not purchase or import phone number lists.

Message Program Details

We use SMS messaging solely for transactional purposes. Messages may include:

  • Account notifications related to our web applications and digital products
  • Updates related to user-submitted requests or data
  • Responses to information you have requested from us

We do not send marketing or promotional SMS messages.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity and requests. We only send messages when there is relevant information to share. This is a low-volume messaging program.

Costs

Msg & data rates may apply. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Please check with your carrier if you are unsure about your messaging rates.

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You can opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. You will receive a single confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. You can re-subscribe at any time by texting us again.

How to Get Help

Reply HELP for assistance or contact us at hello@designdept.com. While the program is inactive, email is the only way to reach us about it.

Consent Is Not a Condition of Purchase

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase or use of our services. You may engage with Design Dept. Partners LLC and use our services without opting in to SMS communications.

Privacy

Your phone number and any data shared via SMS will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not share your phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Terms of Service

Use of our services is subject to our Terms of Service.
